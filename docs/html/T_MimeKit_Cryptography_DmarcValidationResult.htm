Remarks

The result of DMARC validation, as returned by DmarcVerifier.

The result does not decide what to do with the message. The final disposition of a message is a matter of local policy (RFC 9989, Section 5.4): a policy of reject should not be the sole reason to reject a message (RFC 9989, Section 7.4), and local overrides such as trusted forwarders or ARC are up to the caller.

The result also carries the information needed to build DMARC aggregate and failure reports.
