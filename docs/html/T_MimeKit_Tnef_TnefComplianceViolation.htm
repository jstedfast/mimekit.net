public enum TnefComplianceViolation

Remarks

An enumeration of the ways in which a TNEF stream may deviate from the [MS-OXTNEF] and [MS-OXCDATA] specifications, as reported to an ITnefComplianceLogger.

A handful of members do not describe a defect in the stream at all, but rather a limit that the stream exceeded. They are reported the same way so that a consumer can tell that some of the content was not processed.
